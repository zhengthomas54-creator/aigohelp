Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

At AIGoHelp, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the AIGoHelp website and its online tools.

AIGoHelp is designed to provide simple online tools without requiring users to create an account for basic use.

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect or receive certain types of information, including:

  • Information you voluntarily provide, such as information submitted through a contact form, if available.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location, and IP address, which may be collected automatically by hosting, security, analytics, or other technical services.
  • Information about how you interact with the website, such as pages visited, approximate visit duration, and general usage patterns, where analytics tools are enabled.

2. Information You Enter Into Our Tools

Some AIGoHelp tools allow you to enter information in order to generate or process content.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information, passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other highly confidential information into AIGoHelp tools unless the tool specifically requires it and clearly states how that information is handled.

The way information is processed may vary depending on the specific tool. Please review the information displayed on the relevant tool page before submitting sensitive information.

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating AIGoHelp tools.
  • Improving the functionality, performance, and usability of the website.
  • Monitoring website performance and understanding general usage trends.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing security issues, abuse, or technical problems.
  • Responding to requests submitted by users.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

AIGoHelp may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, analytics, and other legitimate purposes.

Third-party services used by AIGoHelp may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

You can generally control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Analytics

AIGoHelp may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website.

Analytics information may include pages viewed, traffic sources, device information, approximate geographic information, and general interaction patterns.

Analytics services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies when appropriate.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to provide services, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

AIGoHelp is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technologies, legal requirements, or business practices.

13. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of AIGoHelp, please use the contact method provided on the AIGoHelp website.
